Numi Packaging

Sustainable packaging design for organic tea products

Project Summary

Numi Packaging is a sustainable packaging design project focused on creating eco-friendly packaging solutions for organic tea products. The design emphasizes natural materials, minimal environmental impact, and clear product communication while maintaining premium brand positioning.
